All planned leaves must be communicated in advance and approved by your reporting manager. In cases of emergencies or unplanned absences, it is your responsibility to inform your manager and the HR department as soon as possible, preferably at the start of the working day.
Everyone must know how to use the Approval app in Microsoft Teams for applying leaves. Please reach out to the HR team if you need guidance on how to use it.
Try to avoid taking leaves unless absolutely necessary, as frequent or unplanned absences can significantly impact your performance or LOP and team productivity. Uninformed or unapproved absences may lead to disciplinary action, including formal warnings.
Note:
Emergency leaves must be informed within 24 hours.
Casual leaves must be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance.
We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a professional and respectful workplace. If you have any questions or need clarification regarding the leave policy, please feel free to contact the HR team.
Thank you for your attention and commitment.
